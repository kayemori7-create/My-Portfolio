Bookkeeping Work Sample

A set of Xero-generated reports produced during a 30-day remote bookkeeping internship at Digital Bytes Financial Consultancy Services — covering financial statements, tax compliance, receivables & payables, and transaction records.

Xero Australian BAS/IAS Bank Reconciliation Financial Reporting

Context: These reports were produced for a training-client file during the internship (Jul–Aug 2026), covering the period 1 January–31 March 2024. They demonstrate the full quarterly bookkeeping cycle in Xero: monthly and quarterly financial statements, GST/BAS and PAYG/IAS activity statements, aged receivables and payables tracking, and full transaction-level records.

Financial Statements

Core monthly and quarterly reporting: profitability, financial position, and a plain-language executive summary of both.

BAS & IAS Activity Statements

GST and PAYG reporting to the Australian Tax Office, plus the supporting audit reports that reconcile every figure back to source transactions.

Receivables & Payables

Ageing summaries used to track who owes the business money, and what the business owes its suppliers.

Transaction Records

Detailed, line-by-line records: every journal entry and every posted transaction across all accounts for the quarter.
